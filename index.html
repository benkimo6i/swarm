Swarm

A swarm of AI agents.
One team that moves as one.

Swarm is a workforce of AI agents that plan, build, test and ship together. Every agent takes a role, from product manager to site reliability engineer, and picks up work the moment it appears. No tickets waiting in a queue. No handoffs lost between teams. No one blocked on anyone else. You set a direction and the swarm is already heading there, each agent adjusting to the ones around it. You point. They move as one.
